Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 30, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how TensorixAI HR Automation ("the application", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when the application is connected to LinkedIn for publishing content to the TensorixAI LinkedIn Company Page.

The application is an internal automation system used to prepare, review, and publish company social-media content.

2. Information We Process

When the application is authorized to use LinkedIn, it may process information required to authenticate the authorized LinkedIn account and publish content on behalf of the TensorixAI LinkedIn Company Page.

Depending on the LinkedIn authorization and API functionality used, this may include:

  • LinkedIn account or member identification information required for authorization
  • LinkedIn organization or company-page identification
  • Access tokens required to communicate with LinkedIn APIs
  • Content prepared for publication, including captions, hashtags, images, and related media
  • Publishing results and API responses required for operational logging

The application does not intentionally request or collect unrelated LinkedIn member information.

3. How We Use Information

Information processed through the LinkedIn integration is used to:

  • Authenticate an authorized LinkedIn user
  • Confirm authorization to publish on behalf of the TensorixAI Company Page
  • Upload approved media to LinkedIn
  • Publish approved company content
  • Record publishing status and technical results
  • Troubleshoot and maintain the automation workflow

Content is not published automatically until it has passed the application's human approval process.

4. Human Approval

The application includes a human approval step before company content is published.

Authorized reviewers can approve or reject generated content. Approval records may include the reviewer identifier, review timestamp, decision, and review comment.

Rejected content is not sent to the publishing stage.

5. Data Storage

The HR Automation Platform uses self-hosted infrastructure for its application workflow and database.

LinkedIn-related technical information required for the integration may be stored in the application's secure configuration or database where necessary for operation.

Access tokens and other credentials should be protected and are not intentionally displayed publicly or included in application source code.

6. Data Sharing

The application does not sell personal information.

Information is shared with LinkedIn only as necessary to perform the authorized LinkedIn integration, including authentication, media upload, and content publishing.

The application does not intentionally share unrelated employee or internal HR information with LinkedIn.

7. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate, maintain, troubleshoot, and audit the automation workflow, or as otherwise required by applicable obligations.

LinkedIn access can be revoked by the authorized account through the applicable LinkedIn controls.

8. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect application data and credentials.

Access to the automation platform and publishing functionality is controlled through authentication and role-based authorization.

No security measure can guarantee absolute security.

9. Third-Party Services

The application uses LinkedIn APIs to provide company-page publishing functionality.

Use of LinkedIn is also subject to LinkedIn's applicable terms and privacy policies.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application's functionality, data processing practices, or applicable requirements change.

The effective date shown at the beginning of this policy indicates the current version.

11. Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy or the TensorixAI HR Automation LinkedIn integration, contact the TensorixAI organization through its official business contact channel or LinkedIn Company Page.
